# Episode 01 — Owner Review Checklist

This checklist applies to the private preview generated by BookFlow. A successful render is a technical pass, not creative approval.

## Files expected
- Long episode: MP4, 1920×1080, exactly 300 seconds, silent.
- Companion Short: MP4, 1080×1920, 48 seconds, silent.

## Review gates
- [ ] Both MP4 assets exist in the private draft GitHub Release.
- [ ] Arabic text is correctly shaped, readable, and not clipped on a phone.
- [ ] English headline and Arabic translation are paired and legible.
- [ ] The picture changes meaningfully every 2–3 seconds; no repeated static-card sequence.
- [ ] Scene motifs match the story beat and do not substitute for real documentary footage where footage is needed.
- [ ] Ronald Read's estate is described as nearly/about $8 million, not as a bank balance.
- [ ] The $4.8m hospital and $1.2m library gifts are not confused with the total estate.
- [ ] Richard Fuscone's bankruptcy is contextualized without claiming one exclusive cause.
- [ ] No unsupported statistics, invented quotations, or copied book passages.
- [ ] Silent audio behavior remains as configured; no music, narration, or SFX added without owner approval.

## Approval policy
A green workflow, valid MP4 metadata, or private draft release does not equal creative approval. The owner must watch both files and explicitly approve before any separate publishing workflow is considered. BookFlow itself never uploads or publishes to YouTube or TikTok.

## Current quality note
The renderer includes scene-specific illustrated motifs (worker, executive, civic/community, foreclosure, time, growth, ledger, and book). These are vector illustrations, not photorealistic B-roll. Visual quality must be judged from the actual MP4s before calling the episode final.
